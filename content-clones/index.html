How it works

1You send the real you. Your own photos, a 2–3 minute recording of your voice reading our script, and your written consent. No consent, no clone — ever.
2We build your face from your photo. One step from the real photo, then careful edits only — never a copy of a copy, so it stays you.
3You approve the face. Nothing is animated until you look at the still and say "that's me."
4We build your voice. Your recording becomes the master; our own license-clean engines clone it, and you hear a test line and approve the voice before full production.
5Your clone goes to work. It records your approved scripts and we finish the pieces — edited, polished, ready to post.
6You see it first. Then it's vaulted. Every finished piece passes a full quality check and goes to you before anyone else. The whole production is stored with a tamper-proof fingerprint (SHA-256) so it can always be proven and rebuilt.
