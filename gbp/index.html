Straight answers

Doesn't Google charge for a listing?

No — a Google Business Profile itself is free from Google. What you pay us for is the work: claiming it, building it out right, loading it, and fighting through the verification step most owners quit at.

What do I have to do?

One thing: shoot one short verification video on your phone, proving the business is real. Google requires it — and I walk you through it step by step, the same way I got my own business verified.

How long does it take?

Most profiles are built and ready to submit within 48 hours of your verification video. After that, verification timing is Google's call — the build and the walk-through are ours.

Do I keep my profile?

Yes. The profile is your business's, in your name — we build it and hand it back to you complete. No hostage listings, ever.

Will this put me #1 on Google?

Nobody honest can promise that, and we won't. What we promise is a complete, verified, correctly built profile — the thing Google actually rewards — instead of an unclaimed listing sending jobs to the next shop.

Is there a contract?

No. The setup and the bundle are one-time prices. Review management is month to month — first month free when you add it to a setup — and you can stop it any month.
