The short version

Hustle City 2 collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no server. The app works fully offline.

What the app stores, and where

The game saves your best score in storage on your own device, inside the app. That information never leaves your device — the app contains no code that transmits data anywhere, and it requests no network permission. The one external link in the game (our support page) opens in your browser only if you tap it; what your browser does after that is governed by your browser, not by this app.

What we collect

  • Personal data: none
  • Usage / analytics data: none
  • Device identifiers: none
  • Location: none
  • Contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera: never accessed

Sharing

We share nothing with anyone, because we receive nothing. We sell no data — there is no data to sell.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores lives on your device. Clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app, and it is gone. There is no copy anywhere else to delete.

Children

Hustle City 2 is a fictional game about an illegal street economy and is not directed at children.

Changes

If a future version of this app ever changes what it does with data, this policy will be updated and dated before that version ships.
