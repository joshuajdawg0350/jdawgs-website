The Story That Isn't Over

This is Joshua Dawson's life story, told in his own words and animated in Sin City style — stark black and white, with one true color in every scene. These are the first two scenes. Scene 1: a baby boy is born on an Air Force base in Oscoda, Michigan, in 1977. Scene 2: the family moves to Columbus, Mississippi — and a little boy gets his first ride on the front of his dad's blue motorcycle, in a tiny white helmet.
