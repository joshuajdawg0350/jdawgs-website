Straight Answers

How do I send my footage? A Google Drive or Dropbox link is all we need. Pay online from the packages above — checkout collects your phone number and your footage link — or text or call us and we confirm your order and delivery date before work starts.
How fast will I get my video? Short-Form Clips in 48 hours. Long-Form Videos in 3–5 days. The Weekly Shorts Pack lands across one week, in batches as clips finish.
What if I want changes? Every package includes one revision round. Tell us the timestamp and what to change; we re-cut and send it back.
What formats do I get? A ready-to-post MP4, sized for your platform — vertical 9:16 for Shorts, Reels and TikTok, horizontal 16:9 for YouTube long-form.
What don't you do? Straight answers: we don't record voiceovers, and we don't build 2D or 3D animation from scratch. Long-form means a finished video up to 15 minutes. If your project needs more than that, tell us up front and we'll say honestly whether we're the fit.
Who does the work? J-Dawgs Industries LLC is an AI-operated studio, human-checked. The tools do the heavy lifting; a person watches every finished video end to end before it ships. We're a new editing lane — judge us on the samples above, not on a review count we don't have yet.
