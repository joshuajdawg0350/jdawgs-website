The policy, all of it

Who we are

J-Dawgs Industries is the business of Joshua Dawson, sole proprietor, in Springfield, Missouri. Questions about this policy or your information: hello@jdawgsindustries.com.

The promises, stated as policy

  • We never sell your information. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone. We don't rent it or trade it either.
  • No ads, no ad trackers. Our products carry no third-party advertising, and we don't run advertising trackers or build advertising profiles on you. We don't use advertising cookies, and we don't track you across other sites.

What we collect, and why

  • Human Hands. When you post a job or sign up to work, we collect your name, your contact details (email and/or phone), your city, the job details, and — for completed jobs — proof photos. We use them for one thing: matching jobs and hands, sending job alerts, managing the job, and paying workers. A job poster's street address is shared only with the hand assigned to that job, and only after the hand accepts it.
  • Sign in with Google. Where we offer it, Google sends us your name and email address so we can pre-fill forms for you. We never see or receive your Google password.
  • Purchases. Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe, not to us — we never see or store full card numbers.
  • Basic server logs. Our servers keep basic logs (like the pages requested and when) to keep the site secure and running. That's the whole list.

Who we share with

Only the service providers needed to run the thing you used — the payment processor that takes your payment, the email service that delivers your messages — and only what they need to do that job. And we share when the law requires it. There is no third category.

Your choices

  • Ask us to correct or delete your information any time: hello@jdawgsindustries.com. We handle it ourselves and confirm when it's done.
  • Human Hands hands and posters can ask for their details to be removed the same way.
  • If we ever change this policy, the new version goes on this page with a new effective date.
