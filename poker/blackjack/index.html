BLACKJACK

Blackjack against Midas, the house dealer — 10 hands, play chips, no account, no money. He grades every decision you make against basic strategy. After hand 10, Midas tells you exactly what he saw in your game.

How to play — tap to open
  1. You start with 1,000 play chips. Before each hand, pick your bet: 10, 25, 50, or 100. If your stack drops below the minimum bet, it tops back up to 1,000 — play chips, the session always goes the full 10 hands.
  2. You get two cards; Midas deals himself two, one face up. Beat his hand by getting closer to 21 without going over. Face cards count 10; an ace counts 1 or 11, whichever helps.
  3. Blackjack — ace plus a ten-value card on the deal — pays 3:2. Midas stands on all 17s. There's no insurance bet; we keep it clean.
  4. Your moves: Hit (take a card), Stand (stay), Double (double your bet, take exactly one card), and Split (a pair becomes two hands — aces get one card each). Every move you make is graded against basic strategy — Midas is reading how you play, not just what you hold.
  5. Play all 10 hands. After hand 10, you get Midas's Read on your blackjack game — and an invite into our poker room.
