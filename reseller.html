1 · What this is

There are two ways to resell. A reseller picks one per product line and can run both: Tier A sells through our checkout with a unique code; Tier B is a white-label license to sell under your own name through your own checkout.

3 · Tier A — Standard Reseller (our checkout, your code)

The buyer buys through our checkout using your unique reseller code or link. We hold the money, the receipt, and the customer relationship. You get a sales report with every settlement.

  • Digital goods: you keep 40% of the sale price.
  • Services: you keep 25% of the first payment, 20% of any renewal payments on subscriptions.

Why these numbers: digital goods cost us nothing extra to deliver, so the seller who brought the buyer takes a big share — 40% is deliberately above the 20–30% most affiliate programs pay. Services are our labor — studio time, editing, running proof rigs — so the cut is smaller, and renewals pay less than the first sale because the reseller's work was bringing the customer once. On Tier A we absorb the payment-processor fees out of our share.

Customer: ours. The buyer bought from J-Dawgs; you receive counts and amounts, not the customer's name or contact details.

4 · Tier B — White-Label License (your checkout, your name)

For the agent room and for agencies. You sell under your own name, through your own checkout, at list price or above — never below list without our written OK. Nobody undercuts the shelf.

  • The split: our wholesale price is 60% of list. You remit 60% of list per sale to J-Dawgs monthly and keep everything above it. Sell at list and you keep 40%; price above list and the extra is entirely yours. You absorb your own payment-processor fees.

Why this shape: a white-label seller owns the storefront and the customer, does the marketing, and carries their own checkout costs — so they keep the whole margin above a fixed wholesale floor. The floor is 60% of list, so a Tier B seller at list price earns exactly what a Tier A seller earns; the tiers differ in who owns the customer, not in who gets the better deal.

Customer: yours. For services, you pass us only the details needed to do the work. The end buyer's guarantee rights (Section 5) apply no matter whose name is on the storefront.

Limits: the license is per-seller and revocable (Section 7). You may not sublicense it, resell the license itself, or let a third party sell under your license. Hosted by Joshua is not available on Tier B — see Section 2.

5 · Name protection — the heart of these terms

These rules are not negotiable, and breaking one ends the deal (Section 7).

  1. The Fix-It Guarantee flows through unchanged. Every end buyer, on either tier, gets the same guarantee they'd get buying from us directly: if it doesn't work, we fix it or refund it. We back it — the work and the cost are ours. You never weaken, reword, or hide it.
  2. Our refund policy applies exactly. You honor refunds on the same terms we do. On Tier B, you process the buyer's refund at your checkout and we reverse our wholesale share of that sale.
  3. Refunded sales don't pay. If a sale is refunded or charged back, the reseller's cut on it reverses against the next settlement. No exceptions.
  4. The proof law binds resellers. Nothing may be sold as "verified," "proven," or "tested" unless a public proof page for that product is live on jdawgsindustries.com — and when you use a proof claim, you link the proof page. Current proof pages govern: the AI Tool Store proof page covers the 97 tools that passed a real run on 2026-11-16 — the 3 products that failed were pulled and may not be sold at all until they are fixed, re-proven, and restored.
  5. No invented anything. No fake stats, customers, testimonials, earnings, or results — ours or yours. If a number isn't on a public page of ours or in your own settlement report, you can't say it.
  6. No claiming the build. You sell J-Dawgs products; you did not make them. On Tier B you may sell under your own brand, but you may not claim authorship of the product, and anything that carries the J-Dawgs name follows the house brand rules (chrome/silver + electric blue on near-black, "Big dreams for a small world," no reworded guarantee).
  7. No junk behavior. No spam, no fake engagement, no bait pricing, no selling to audiences the product doesn't fit. One warning for a gray area; none for a clear violation.

6 · Mechanics

  • Joining: apply through the Service Request Door — name the Reseller Program, your storefront or audience, and which tier and products you want. We answer every real application with a yes and a code, or a plain no.
  • Codes: Tier A resellers get a unique code/link per product line. Tier B sellers get a license ID that goes on every monthly report.
  • Settlement: monthly, for the prior month, paid by the 15th via Stripe or PayPal (reseller's choice at signup). No minimum sales to join or stay. Payouts under $10 roll forward to the next month — that's a settlement rule, not a barrier.
  • Reporting: Tier A — we send the sales report. Tier B — you send a monthly sales report with your remittance; we can audit it against delivery records for services (every service sale creates a job on our side, so service counts are checkable both ways).
  • Taxes & status: resellers are independent sellers, not employees, agents, or partners of J-Dawgs. Each side handles its own taxes.
  • No exclusivity. We can appoint other resellers; you can sell other makers' products. Nobody owns a territory.

7 · Term and termination

  • The arrangement runs month to month. Either side may end it at any time with written notice (email or door message is fine).
  • We may cut a reseller off immediately for violating Section 5 — false claims, guarantee tampering, junk behavior — or for non-payment of Tier B remittances. Cut-off stops new sales under the code/license at once.
  • Sales made cleanly before cut-off still settle in full on the normal schedule. End buyers keep their guarantee regardless — a reseller's behavior never costs a buyer their Fix-It coverage.

8 · The plain fine print

Prices and the catalog can change; changes apply to new sales, never retroactively to settled ones, and resellers get notice before a price change hits their links. If any line of these terms conflicts with a signed written agreement, the signed agreement wins.
