Placed means proven — or it doesn't count. Two tools in one. Proof Sheet: give it a placements list (Venue, What was placed, Live URL) and it fetches every URL back. Weekly Report: give it a metrics list (Venue, Placements, Clicks, Signups, Sales, Revenue) and it writes the weekly performance report with totals.
