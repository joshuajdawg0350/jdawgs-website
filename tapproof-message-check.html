The four moves every scam message makes

Pretend to be someone you trust. Invent a problem or a prize. Pressure you to act now. Demand payment one specific way (the FTC's four signs). One sign can be a coincidence. Three together is a script.

Whatever the message says: don't tap its links, don't reply, don't call its number. Forward texts to 7726 (SPAM), then check the claim inside the company's real app or on the number printed on your card.
