How to read any link yourself

Read the name right to left: in login.usps.com.evil-site.top, the owner is evil-site.top — everything before it is decoration. The genuine article is short: usps.com. One changed letter (usps-track.com, paypa1.com) means a different owner entirely.

The standing rule: never reach a bank, carrier, or agency through a message link. Open their app, or type the address you already know.
