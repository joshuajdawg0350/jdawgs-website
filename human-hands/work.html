The deal, in plain words

What you agree to when you sign up

Signing up is free. You must be 18 or older and have a smartphone. You keep 100% of the posted pay and 100% of any tips — the poster pays a flat 15% fee on top, and it never comes out of your pay. You send the photo proof the job asks for, the poster approves it, and then you get paid. Signing up holds your roster spot — it is not a guarantee of jobs or earnings; jobs are matched as people post them.

How jobs reach you: When a job posts that matches your tier and the work you signed up for, we email you a job alert with the full details of the job and the pay — everything except the exact address. The address is never in the alert. To take a job, reply YES to the alert — the first yes gets the job, and only then do we send you the exact address. By replying YES you agree to do the job as described and send the proof it asks for (a photo, where the job requires one). You keep 100% of the posted pay and 100% of any tips.
