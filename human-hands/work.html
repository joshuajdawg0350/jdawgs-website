The deal, in plain words

What you agree to when you sign up

Signing up is free. You must be 18 or older and have a smartphone. You keep 100% of the posted pay and 100% of any tips — the poster pays a flat 15% fee on top, and it never comes out of your pay. You send the photo proof the job asks for, the poster approves it, and then you get paid. Signing up holds your roster spot — it is not a guarantee of jobs or earnings; jobs are matched as people post them.

How jobs reach you: When a job posts that matches your tier and the work you told us you want (in your reply to our welcome email), we email you a job alert with the full details of the job and the pay — everything except the exact address. The address is never in the alert. To take a job, reply YES to the alert — the first yes gets the job, and only then do we send you the exact address. By replying YES you agree to do the job as described and send the proof it asks for (a photo, where the job requires one). You keep 100% of the posted pay and 100% of any tips.

Worker questions

Plain answers, before you sign up

Does it cost anything to sign up?

No. Signing up is free — no registration fee, no background-check fee, no cut taken out of your pay. The poster pays a flat 15% fee on top of the posted price; it never comes out of what you earn.

When do I get paid?

You send the photo proof the job asks for, the person who posted the job approves it, and then you get paid — 100% of the posted price, plus 100% of any tip.

Do I have to take every job I'm offered?

No. Job alerts come by email with the details and the pay. Reply YES only to the ones you want — the first yes gets the job. There is no schedule and no quota.

Is work guaranteed?

No — and we'll say it plainly: signing up holds your spot on the founding roster, it is not a guarantee of jobs or earnings. Jobs are matched as people in the Springfield area post them.
