The details

What this job covers

What moving help covers: loading a rental truck or trailer, unloading at the new place, carrying furniture and boxes up or down stairs, and moving one heavy item across town or across the room. Say in the posting what is heaviest, how many flights of stairs there are, and how many Hands you need — the posting spells it out, and Hands claim work that fits them.

You run your move: the Hands are the labor, on your schedule and your plan — you have the truck, the keys, and the list of what goes where. The Hands show up at the time in your posting and do the lifting.

Matched by tier: plain carrying is open work any Hand 18+ can claim. If part of the move needs tools or experience — taking a door off its hinges, breaking down a bunk bed — say so in the posting and it matches as skilled work instead.

Proof and pay: when the work is done, the Hand sends photo proof — the loaded truck, the emptied room — and you approve the proof before the Hand is paid.
