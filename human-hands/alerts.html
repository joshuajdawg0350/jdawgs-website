Put the app on your home screen

The app opens straight to the job board — no browser, no typing the address. On Android it also makes alerts more reliable.

On an Android phone:
  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app (on some phones it says Add to Home screen).
  3. Tap Install. The Human Hands app is now on your home screen.
  4. Open it from your home screen and turn alerts on here.
On an iPhone: alerts only work inside the installed app, so do this first —
  1. Tap the Share button in Safari (the square with an arrow).
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open the Human Hands app from your home screen and turn alerts on here.
