The details

What this job covers

What a wait covers: furniture and appliance deliveries, the repair tech, the cable or internet installer, the plumber's four-hour window — any appointment where an adult has to be home or the visit doesn't happen at all. The Hand can sign for the delivery, bring a package inside, stay while the work is done, and lock up after.

How you stay in the loop: your instructions go right in the posting — where to meet them, what to sign for, what photo you want back. The Hand sends photo proof: the delivered item in place, the signed slip, the finished install. You approve the proof before the Hand is paid.

Why not just reschedule: a missed delivery usually means days more of waiting and another day off work to try again. A posted wait costs the price you set plus the one flat fee — for most people that is cheaper than the lost shift, and the delivery lands the day it was supposed to.

What the Hand needs from you: the window, the address area, and clear instructions. Your exact address is never shown publicly — only the Hand who claims your job gets it.

How it works

Three steps, no mystery

STEP 1

Post the wait

Put the window, the area, and your instructions on the job board and set the price yourself — it takes a couple of minutes.

STEP 2

A Hand claims it

The AI matches your job to a local worker. The first Hand who says yes gets the exact address and your instructions.

STEP 3

Proof it happened

The Hand waits, signs, and photographs the delivery or the finished work. You approve the proof, and the Hand gets paid.

The price, in plain words

You set the price. One flat fee on top.

Post a delivery wait at $30 — you pay $34.50 total, the Hand gets the full $30, plus 100% of any tip you add. That flat 15% on top is the whole cost: no subscription, no contract, no minimums, no other fees. And if no worker matches your job, your payment is refunded in full automatically — cancelling before work starts is a full refund too.
