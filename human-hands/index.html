How it works

Three steps, no mystery

STEP 1

Post the job

Say what needs doing and set the price yourself. Post it on the job board — it takes a couple of minutes.

STEP 2

The AI makes the match

The AI matches your job to a local worker by skill, tier and distance. That's the whole AI part — making the perfect match, fast and efficiently.

STEP 3

A person does the work

A real local person shows up, does the job, and sends back photo proof. You approve the proof, and the worker gets paid.

The deal, in plain words

Win. Win. Win.

You set the price per job — say a property check posted at $25. The worker keeps the full $25, plus 100% of any tip. You add a flat 20% on top, and that's the whole cost: no subscription, no contract, no minimums. You pick the level the job needs: Tier 1 — Open (any worker 18+ with a smartphone), Tier 2 — Skilled (tools and experience), or Tier 3 — Licensed (licenses verified). Paying is one tap from your phone — tap to pay with Apple Pay, Google Pay, or Link, whichever your phone offers.

Example totals, with the 20% already added: a $25 job → $30.00 · a $30 job → $36.00 · a $35 job → $42.00 · an $80 job → $96.00. Examples only — you always set the price. And one fee is the whole fee stack: no service fee plus a trust fee plus a lead fee buried in the quote.

No match, no charge: if no worker matches your job, your payment is refunded in full automatically. Cancel before work starts for a full refund. Full policy: https://jdawgsindustries.com/refunds.html

Questions

Plain answers

What kinds of jobs can I post?

Regular work people have always needed done: checking on a vacant rental between tenants, lockbox photos for a sale, storm-damage photos after a storm, pickups and deliveries, in-person verification visits, waiting at a house for a delivery, install and assembly help, yard work, moving help. If a person has to physically be somewhere and do something, it fits.

How does pay work?

You set the price for the job. The worker keeps 100% of that price, plus 100% of any tip you add. A flat 20% platform fee goes on top of the price you set — that's the whole cost, with no subscription, no contract, and no minimums. You approve the photo proof before the worker is paid.

Who are the workers?

Local people, 18 or older with a smartphone — that's the only requirement to sign up. Jobs are matched by tier: open tasks anyone can claim, skilled tasks for people with tools and experience, and licensed work where licenses are verified. Right now the founding roster is 14 of 100 workers, most in the Springfield, Missouri area, and it's growing. Jobs are matched as they come in.

How jobs reach you: When a job posts that matches your tier and the work you signed up for, we email you a job alert with the full details of the job and the pay — everything except the exact address. The address is never in the alert. To take a job, reply YES to the alert — the first yes gets the job, and only then do we send you the exact address. By replying YES you agree to do the job as described and send the proof it asks for (a photo, where the job requires one). You keep 100% of the posted pay and 100% of any tips.

What if something goes wrong?

Every job carries the J-Dawgs Fix-It Guarantee: if anything on our side goes wrong, report it once and we fix it — no hassle. Report a problem or ask a question.

Is this live?

Yes. Human Hands launched on November 10, 2026, in Springfield, Missouri, and matching is open now. We're new and we say so — the roster and the board grow as people post jobs and sign up.
