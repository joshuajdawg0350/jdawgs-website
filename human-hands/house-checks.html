The details

What this job covers

What a house check covers: a vacant rental between tenants (is it secure, any leaks, damage, or things left behind?), lockbox photos for a sale or a new listing, storm-damage photos after weather rolls through Springfield, and move-in / move-out verification — dated photos of every room, the floors, the walls, and the meter, so both sides have a record they can point to.

What you get back: exactly the photos your posting asks for. Say what you need — front and back of the house, each room, a close-up of the lockbox or the meter — and that photo list is the proof the Hand sends back. You approve the proof before the Hand is paid.

Your address stays yours: job alerts show the area and the pay, never your exact street address. Only the Hand who takes your job gets the address, and only after they have claimed it.

Who posts house checks: landlords with a vacant unit, property managers between tenants, sellers and buyers who need eyes on a place before a closing, and anyone with a family home or second property sitting empty across town.

How it works

Three steps, no mystery

STEP 1

Post the check

Say what needs checking and set the price yourself on the job board — the area, the photo list, and when you need it by.

STEP 2

A Hand claims it

The AI matches your job to a local worker. The first Hand who says yes gets the exact address — nobody else sees it.

STEP 3

Photos come back

The Hand visits the property, takes the photos you asked for, and sends them back as proof. You approve the proof, and the Hand gets paid.

The price, in plain words

You set the price. One flat fee on top.

Post a property check at $25 — you pay $28.75 total, the Hand gets the full $25, plus 100% of any tip you add. That flat 15% on top is the whole cost: no subscription, no contract, no minimums, no other fees. And if no worker matches your job, your payment is refunded in full automatically — cancelling before work starts is a full refund too.
