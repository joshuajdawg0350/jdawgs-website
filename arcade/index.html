The screen

Everything gets tested before it lists

No pay-to-skip, no friends-and-family lane. Your work goes through the same screen as ours:

  • Works, start to finish. Games get played end to end. Apps get run through. Movies get watched all the way through — the whole video, no sampling, no skimming. If it breaks, stalls, or dead-ends, it doesn't list.
  • Works where buyers are. Apps and games are checked on real phone sizes, not just a desktop window. Movies are checked on phone and desktop players.
  • The proof run is public. A real run of the real product, posted where anyone can read it — hits and misses both — before any buy link goes live.
  • No ads inside the product. None. That's the whole point of this store.
  • No selling customer data. Your customers' information is never sold — by you or by us.
  • You own the rights. You made it, or you hold the rights to sell it.
  • Not stolen or cloned. No lifted work, no copies wearing a new name.
  • No scams. If it promises one thing and does another, it's out.
  • AI-made is welcome — and labeled. If AI generated your work or helped you make it, say so when you apply. It gets an honest "Made with AI" note on its listing — the same label our own AI-built products carry. Hiding it is the only way AI becomes a problem here.

This is not a taste test. We don't reject work for being weird, dark, or rough around the edges — Weird is welcome. Broken is not.

Fail on something fixable and you get specific notes — what broke, where, on what screen — plus a free re-test once you've fixed it. And the screen doesn't end at listing: buyer complaints trigger a re-test, and a product that breaks and stays broken comes off the shelf.

That's why there's no junk on this shelf.
