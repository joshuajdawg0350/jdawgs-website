Privacy Policy

J-Dawgs Auto Tune Data™ — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 9, 2026 · J-Dawgs Industries, Springfield, MO

The short version: this app collects nothing. There is no server, no account, no analytics, no ads, and nothing you do in the app ever leaves your phone.

What the app does with your information

  • Data collected: None. The app has no sign-up, no user accounts, and no backend. It does not transmit any data anywhere — it does not even request the Android INTERNET permission.
  • Data shared with third parties: None, because nothing is collected.
  • On-device storage: Your vehicle profiles, dyno runs, imported logs, and settings are stored only in the app’s on-device storage, so the app works fully offline. That data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it completely; there is no server copy to delete because there is no server.

Permissions, and exactly why each one exists

  • Bluetooth (nearby devices): used only to connect to your own ELM327 OBD-II adapter when you open Live Gauges and tap Connect. The app talks to the adapter you pick — nothing else, and only while you use that feature.
  • Location: used only on your device, for two things: (1) GPS speed and position during a dyno run, which the app turns into horsepower/torque estimates on the spot; (2) on some Android versions, Bluetooth scanning requires location permission — the scan looks for your adapter only. Your location is never stored anywhere except inside your own saved dyno runs on your device, and it is never transmitted or shared.

Bluetooth data

Live Gauges reads standard OBD-II data (RPM, speed, throttle, timing, fuel trims) from your adapter and displays it to you. The app is read-only: it does not write to, program, or “flash” your vehicle’s computer.

Children

This is a vehicle tool intended for drivers and vehicle owners. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at the same URL as this document before it takes effect.
