Inventory Sheet Builder

Free · Runs on your device · Nothing uploads

Handwritten count sheets become a workbook — but only if the totals prove out. Load a transcription CSV (Item Code, Item Name, Size, Colour, Location, Condition, Quantity — one row per size-colour, as keyed from the paper) and enter the control totals from the paper count itself: how many rows the sheets hold and the total units. The builder validates every quantity, merges duplicate size-colour rows with a flag, never silently, and gates the workbook against your control totals. If the gate fails, the workbook says so on its Checks sheet — a failed gate is a failed delivery, reported, not smoothed over.

The sample is the studio's 12-row test count with a planted duplicate and a planted "abc" quantity: against the true control (10 rows / 276 units) the gate passes; change the quantity control to 999 and watch it fail — exactly as a delivery gate must. Results always appear on this page first; the .xlsx download is a convenience. Once loaded, the tool works offline; your file never leaves your device.
