How to play Pam’s Tiles

  • Drag your finger across touching letters to spell a word — up, down, sideways, or diagonal all work. You can also tap letters one at a time, then tap Check.
  • Each level hides a few words. The empty boxes at the bottom show how many letters each word has.
  • Find all the hidden words to finish the level. Extra real words you find count as bonus words!
  • Stuck? Tap Hint — it’s always free, as many times as you like. Tap Shuffle to mix the board up.

There is no timer, no score you have to beat, and no way to lose. Play at your own pace.
