SEO Metadata Audit

Free · Runs on your device · Nothing uploads

The audit the studio runs before a client's metadata ships. Paste a page's HTML source (in your browser: view-source, select all, copy) and this checks the working limits — title 50–60 characters, meta description 140–160, exactly one H1, alt text on every image — and writes the fix sheet. When something fails, the recommendation is drafted from the page's own H1 and first paragraph. No invented claims, ever: it's a starting draft for a human check, labeled as one.

The sample is a real audit subject — our own Human Hands worker page, saved 2026-11-15: its title (53) and single H1 pass, its 216-character meta description gets flagged with a draft fix. Auditing our own pages with it is the point. Results always appear on this page first; the fix-sheet download is a convenience. Once loaded, the audit works offline; pasted source never leaves your device.
