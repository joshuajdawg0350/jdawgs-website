## How ordering works

1. **Buy the fixed scope.** Pick the service above and pay the price shown at its checkout link. The price on the button is the whole price.
2. **Intake and written scope restatement.** After purchase, you get the intake questions and we restate the scope back to you in writing, with any questions, before work begins. Intake and the written scope restatement arrive the same business day.
3. **Verified delivery, on the stated clock.** Snapshot in 1 business day from the restatement, Research Pack in 3 business days, Deep Pack in 5 business days. Every fact carries its source, and every deliverable passes an independent verification pass before it reaches you.

## The refund rule

If a job falls outside the published scope, the buyer is told in writing and refunded in full **before any work begins** — never routed into an unpriced sales process, never bumped into a larger plan to fit.
