How buying works

Plain dealing, start to finish

Pay your way

Card through the shop's checkout links, Venmo (@Joshua-Dawson-81), or Cash App ($jjdawg0350). Every listing shows its price before you pay anything.

Shipping, stated up front

Cards and coins ship anywhere in the US for $5 flat. The big heavy pieces ship at actual cost, quoted before you pay. One exception, stated plainly: on the pachislo machine, the buyer arranges and pays their own shipping — the shop doesn't quote or arrange it.
