Sell Your Stuff Here — Consignment

Got stuff to sell? Don't want the hassle? Put it on the Trading Post shelf — we photograph it, price it from real sold prices, list it, deal with buyers, pack it and ship it. You get paid when it sells. If it doesn't sell, you pay nothing.

How it works

1

Show us what you've gotEmail photos and a short description (button below). Springfield-area folks can arrange a local drop-off; anyone else can mail items in — you cover getting them to us.

2

We inspect it and agree a price with youPriced from real sold listings, not guesses. The Trading Post can decline any item, for any reason.

3

It goes on the shelfListed like everything else here, marked as a consignment piece.

4

When it sells, you get paidWe pack and ship it — the buyer pays shipping, same as the rest of the shop — and you get your share within 3 business days of the buyer's payment clearing, by Venmo or Cash App.

The split

Items that sell for under $100You keep 65% · Trading Post keeps 35%
Items that sell for $100 or moreYou keep 75% · Trading Post keeps 25%
  • No listing fee. No sale, no fee — ever.
  • Card-processing costs come out of the Trading Post's share, never yours.
  • For comparison: the standard consignment split pays the seller about 60% — most shops keep 40–50% of the sale. The Trading Post pays 65% on everyday items and 75% on bigger ones.
Worked example: Your item sells for $50 → you get $32.50, we keep $17.50. Sells for $200 → you get $150, we keep $50.

The fine print

  • Consignment runs 90 days. If it hasn't sold, you can pick it up (local) or cover return shipping and we'll send it back. Items left unclaimed 30 days after the 90 days end are treated as abandoned and may be donated.
  • You must own the item and it must be legal to sell and ship. No weapons, nothing illegal, nothing that breaks the payment platforms' rules. The Trading Post decides what goes on its shelves.
  • Until it sells, the item is still yours — we just hold and market it. Prices may be adjusted with your OK if something sits.
  • Very small items — anything expected to sell for under about $15 — may be bundled into lots with other small pieces, so the math works for everyone.
