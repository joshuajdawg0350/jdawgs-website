How consignment works — 4 steps

  1. Bring it in (or send a photo). Email or call first with what you've got — cards, coins, collectibles, one-off pieces. If it's a fit, bring it by in Springfield or arrange drop-off.
  2. We do the work. We inspect it, photograph it, price it from sold listings (what the same item actually sold for — not a guess), write the listing, and put it in front of buyers.
  3. It sells. We handle the buyer, take the payment, and ship it — $5 flat on cards and coins anywhere in the US, local Springfield pickup always welcome.
  4. You get paid. Your share lands within 3 business days of cleared payment, by Venmo or Cash App. No sale in the 90-day term? You owe nothing — come get your item, or it's donated as agreed up front.

Straight answers

Trading Post questions, answered plain

What does it cost if my item doesn't sell?

Nothing. No listing fee, and no sale means no fee. If it doesn't sell in the 90-day term, come get it — unclaimed items after the term are treated as abandoned and donated, exactly as agreed when you consign.

When and how do I get paid?

Within 3 business days of the buyer's payment clearing — by Venmo or Cash App, your pick. Not monthly, not "when we get around to it": 3 business days.

How do you price my item?

From sold listings — what the same item actually sold for recently, with condition priced in honestly. Flaws get disclosed in the listing, never hidden, so buyers trust what they read.

What can I consign?

Cards, coins, and collectibles are the core — vintage baseball cards, silver coins, one-off pieces like artwork, framed maps, and game gear. Got something else? Ask first: email joshuajdawg0350@gmail.com or call (417) 324-0216.

Who pays for shipping?

Cards and coins ship anywhere in the US for $5 flat. Heavy pieces ship at actual cost, quoted before anyone pays. One exception, stated plainly: on the pachislo machine, the buyer arranges and pays their own shipping. Local pickup in Springfield is always free and always welcome.

How do I buy something?

Every listing shows its price before you pay anything. Pay by card through the shop's checkout links, Venmo (@Joshua-Dawson-81), or Cash App ($jjdawg0350) — then it ships, or you pick it up in Springfield.
