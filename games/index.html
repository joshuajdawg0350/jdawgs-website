How installing works

Buy → tap to pay → play. Tap to pay with Apple Pay, Google Pay, or Link — whichever your phone offers. No accounts — and no digging through your phone's settings.

Installs like an app — no settings changes

1. Your game page opens the second your payment lands — tap Play and the full game starts.
2. Tap your browser's menu → Add to Home screen.
3. The game's own icon lands on your home screen. It opens full-screen and plays offline — no ads, ever.

Want the Android app package (APK) instead? It's on your download page too. Android asks one permission for downloads like that (Allow from this source) — that's Google's rule for every store, not ours.

Changed phones or lost the page? Your backup copy arrives by email after checkout, and the same link keeps working — just ask and we'll resend it.

On iPhone? Apple doesn't allow direct app installs from any store but theirs — so iPhone players open the same game page and add it to the home screen instead. Same game, same price, still no ads.
