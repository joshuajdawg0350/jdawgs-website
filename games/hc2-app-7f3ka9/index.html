How a run works

You get 45 days, $2,000 cash, and a $2,000 debt to the Shark at 7% a day. Buy cheap in one neighborhood, travel, sell high in another. Traveling or waiting ends the day: interest hits, prices re-roll, the city moves. Banked cash earns 5% a day and can't be mugged. Your coat holds 100 slots (Lean takes 4). Heat brings busts; busts and brawls cost health; zero health is the ambulance. Flat broke with nothing to sell, the Shark offers one $1,000 rescue loan — refuse it and your Charger ends up on blocks. Final score is net worth: cash + bank + stash − debt. Every city is open from day one — travel costs the trip fee and ends the day. Springfield is home: King Balsey's throne is the one unlock that matters, opening at $750,000 to challenge and $1,000,000 to take — and at a million, Balsey's Compound on the Springfield map unlocks too: walk through its gate and you go straight to the hustle-off, street craps for the crown. The market list on the street panel shows every product on the block with live prices and BUY / SELL buttons — trade right there. Or deal face to face: find a dealer standing on the street, open their deal sheet — big simple buttons, dollars printed on every one. Past $10,000 net worth the wholesale lots unlock — pounds of 20 and kilos of 50 at bulk prices — and so does the bank job: once a day, your call. Bail early for a clean $25,000, or see it through on a true 50/50 flip for $250,000. Lose the flip and the run is over. Past $20,000 net worth the BIG MONEY TIER opens for the rest of the run — a 400-slot coat, 100- and 250-unit wholesale lots, a +25% dealer premium on every sell, and a wilder market with richer spikes. That's the ramp: $750,000 challenges the King, $1,000,000 takes the crown.
