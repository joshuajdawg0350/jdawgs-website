The fee comes after the pass, never before — you don't pay a cent to be tested. Once your game passes, you pay the one-time listing fee: $19.99. One payment, per developer, covering every game you list with us — then just 3% of sales. No renewal, no surprise charges later.

Listed

The screen

Every game gets tested before it lists

No pay-to-skip, no friends-and-family lane. Your game goes through the same screen as ours:

  • Works, start to finish. We play it end to end. If it breaks, stalls, or dead-ends, it doesn't list.
  • Works on phones. Real phone sizes, not just a desktop window — that's where most players are.
  • No ads inside the game. None. That's the whole point of this store.
  • No selling player data. Your players' information is never sold — by you or by us.
  • You own the rights. You made it, or you hold the rights to sell it.
  • Not stolen or cloned. No lifted games, no copies wearing a new name.
  • No scams. If it promises one thing and does another, it's out.

This is not a taste test. We don't reject games for being weird, dark, or rough around the edges — Weird is welcome. Broken is not.

Fail on something fixable and you get specific notes — what broke, where, on what screen — plus a free re-test once you've fixed it. And the screen doesn't end at listing: buyer complaints trigger a re-test, and a game that breaks and stays broken comes off the shelf.

That's why there's no junk on this shelf.

Confidential

Your game stays yours

Everything you share with us is confidential — your game, your code, your builds, your ideas. All of it.

We use a submission for one thing only: to test it and list it. We will never steal, copy, clone, or shop around anything you send us — not your game, not your idea, from anybody. That's not a policy we bend; that's a promise we keep.

And if your game doesn't pass the screen, we keep nothing and use nothing. It goes back to you, and it's like it was never here.

Your work is yours. Our job is to sell it, not take it.

House rules

The rules, up front

  • Confidential. What you submit is used only to test and list it — never copied, never stolen, never shared.
  • You own the rights. You must own your game, or hold the rights to sell it. No exceptions.
  • No ads in-game. Listed games run no ads — that's the whole point of this store.
  • No selling player data. Your players' information is theirs. We never sell it, and neither do you.
  • The Fix-It Guarantee covers the store side. If our store breaks your sale — a bad link, a broken page — we fix it free. Your game's bugs stay yours to fix.
  • Broken or dishonest gets delisted. If a listed game stops working or wasn't what it claimed to be, we can take it down.
