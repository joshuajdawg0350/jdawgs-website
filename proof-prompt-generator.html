What it does badly — the misses, with evidence

Miss 1 — it does not clean up your punctuation (Run 7). Type the way people actually type, with periods, and they pass straight through: the output literally reads "Wide establishing shot of a lighthouse keeper., on the rocks, at dusk., calm. mood, …" — stray ".," pairs included. The facts and non-negotiables fields get careful period handling; the subject, setting, and mood fields do not.
Miss 2 — it appends the word "mood" to your mood (Runs 2 and 7). Type "moody" and the prompt says "moody mood"; type "calm." and it says "calm. mood". It never checks whether you already said it.
Miss 3 — the people recipe is applied to everything (Run 2). A prompt for a storefront — no person in the scene — still carries "lifelike skin with visible pores, fine lines and stubble … realistic catchlights in the eyes." The detail sets are written for people and are applied to any subject.
Miss 4 — it can repeat itself (Run 4). With the top realism detail level in prose formats, the phrase "lifelike detail throughout" appears twice in one prompt — once inside the detail recipe and once from the sentence wrapped around it.
Miss 5 — blank in, generic out, no warning (Run 6). Leave every field empty and it silently substitutes "a lone figure" and its own default light and camera. The output is usable — but nothing in it tells you defaults were used.
Miss 6 — no length discipline (Run 8). A 340-character rambling subject passes through whole, and the setting is appended even when the subject already said it ("…the back porch of the family farmhouse…" followed by ", the back porch"). Output quality depends on input discipline; the generator will not shorten, dedupe, or push back.

The honest limit, stated plainly: this generator is a deterministic recipe — tested prompt language assembled by template. The same inputs produce the same outputs, every time. It does not call an AI model and it does not judge your idea; "expert-grade" means the recipe was built and tested by us, not that a model reviewed your prompt.
