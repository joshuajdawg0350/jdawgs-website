How it works

One report. We handle the rest.

1 · Tell us once

Use the report form or email us. Say what's broken and where you got it. No receipt, no order number, no proof of purchase required.

2 · We fix it

Reports land where the operator works them. If the fault is on our side — a bug, a broken button, a bad file — the fix is free. That's the whole policy.

3 · You hear back

When it's fixed, you get told it's fixed. The guarantee is public on purpose: if we ship junk, it's on us, in the open.

Already proven in public: the first ticket on the board was a dead buy button in Hustle City, found and fixed the same day. When the Ringtone & Alert Maker's microphone failed the founder's own phone test, the fixed build replaced the file buyers download — same link. That's what the guarantee is for.
