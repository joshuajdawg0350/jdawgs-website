The launch story

Humans Hire the AI. The AI Does the Hiring.

On November 10, 2026, J-Dawgs Industries launches Human Hands, a job marketplace run through AI agents: a person hires an AI agent to handle a real-world job; the agent does the hiring — finding, booking and paying the best-matched worker — and the work gets done through human hands. Workers keep 100% of the posted price; the payer adds a 15% platform fee. The only requirements to work are 18+ and a smartphone. Human Hands also launches on Product Hunt the same day.

"I build houses for a living. I know what it's like to need a job done and have no way to get it done. Now people can hand that problem to their AI — the AI finds the help, books it, and the person who shows up keeps every dollar of the posted price. That's all this is. Nobody's renting anybody."

— Joshua Dawson, founder, J-Dawgs Industries (from the launch release)
