The Read on YOU

Paste your poker hand histories. Get back a plain-words read on how you actually play — what you do on every street, where your chips leak, and exactly how a smart opponent would play against you.

Your hands never leave your browser. This page does all the work right here on your device. Nothing is uploaded, sent, or saved anywhere. Close the tab and it is gone.

How it works

1. Copy about 50 hands you played, in the simple format below. (Fewer hands still works — the read just labels itself PRELIMINARY and tells you how many hands it stands on.)
2. Paste them in the box and type your player name exactly as it appears in the hands.
3. Press the button. Your read appears below — no account, no upload, no waiting.

The format

One hand per block. Cards look like As Kd Qh 10c (rank, then s/h/d/c for the suit). The amount on a raise or bet is the total bet TO on that street; the amount on a call is what the call puts in. Streets come from the FLOP: / TURN: / RIVER: lines.

HAND
PLAYERS: Alice, Bob, Carol
HERO: Alice
HOLE Alice: As Kd
ACTION Alice raise 200
ACTION Bob call
FLOP: Qs Jh 2c
ACTION Alice check
ACTION Bob bet 150
ACTION Alice call 150
TURN: 7d
ACTION Bob check
ACTION Alice bet 300
ACTION Bob fold
RIVER: 3s
RESULT: Alice
